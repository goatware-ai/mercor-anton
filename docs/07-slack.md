# 🧑‍💻 Slack

## Slack Guidelines & Usage

**Slack is the primary communication channel for Imperium | SWE Trajectory QC Annotation** — all major updates, task clarifications, and project-related discussions will be shared there. Please check Slack regularly, as this is where feedback, reminders, and important announcements will be posted.

**Step 1: Join the Slack Workspace**

Join our project's Slack workspace through the Okta setup on work.mercor.com after signing your contract. Refer to the guide here.

### Step 2: Set Your Display Name

Go to Okta dashboard

1. Click your name (top right corner) & click "Account Settings"
2. Click the "Edit Profile" button
3. Click the "Edit" button in the "Personal Information" section
4. In the Display name field, input your updated display name
5. Click "Save" & the changes will propagate through to Slack (may take a bit)

### Step 3: Join Required Channels

Upon joining Slack, you should be automatically added to the channels listed below.

*Every writer and reviewer must be in the following channels:*

**🔔 #anton-announcements-bf1**

- This channel is for official updates and organizational announcements from SPLs and EPMs that impact the entire project. 🚫 **Please turn on all notifications for this channel**

**🧰 #anton-tooling-support-bf1**

- This channel is for troubleshooting issues related to project tools (e.g., Mercor Studio access, submission forms, or technical setup). If your issue persists for more than 24 hours without a response, DM **@Aahil Ali**on Slack.

**🌐 #anton-writers-5d4**

- **Upon integration into "Imperium | SWE Trajectory QC Annotation" slack please take a second to introduce yourself in this channel**
- This channel is a more casual setting for informal conversations, resource sharing, and building connections with teammates outside of work discussions.

### Communication Guidelines

- Use **channels** for instances where the whole team can benefit from the shared answers.
  - General project queries → **#anton-general-bf1**
  - Tool or setup issues → **#anton-tooling-support-bf1**
- If you have not received a response within 24 hours, ping the relevant team member directly on Slack.
- Collaborate constructively with your reviewer when you receive feedback. Both of you share responsibility for delivering high-quality, on-time annotations.Please refrain from direct messaging project leadership (SPL, EPM, TPM) unless your question or concern hasn't been addressed in the channels.

### Why We Use Channels

Posting in public channels ensures:

- Transparency and visibility for project managers.
- Creation of an evolving **FAQ** for common issues.
- Faster support — often, another team member might answer before staff do.

We **encourage open communication** and questions, it's better to ask in a channel than to stay uncertain. If a question is personal or confidential, feel free to contact any of the project leads.
