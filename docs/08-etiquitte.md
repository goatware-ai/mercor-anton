- **Do not hoard tasks.** Please do not have multiple tasks in the **“Claimed”** state at the same time. If this continues to happen, we will:
- Unassign the extra claimed tasks, and
- Take further action if the behavior persists.
- Hoarding tasks prevents others from having a fair opportunity to contribute to the project.
- **Keep communication professional.** If you have general questions, please post them in **#anton-general-bf1** unless someone has specifically asked you to DM them. The leadership team receives a high volume of direct messages, so using the shared channel helps us respond more efficiently and ensures answers benefit everyone.
- **Prioritize quality over speed.** Your responses should meet our quality standards. Rushing to maximize throughput at the expense of quality may result in removal from the project. Please remember that task-based compensation only applies to tasks that are approved and delivered to the client. For example, completing 50 low-quality tasks that cannot be used will not result in payment for those tasks. **Your annotations must be your own work. Use models only to collect the rollouts the task calls for - never to write your labels, rationale, or evidence. Bonuses reward original, carefully reasoned annotations; LLM-generated ones are rejected and forfeit eligibility.**
