# ❓ FAQ

## FAQ

> **Q: How does payment on the project work?**

**A:** Writers are compensated on a per-task basis, while reviewers are compensated on an hourly basis.

Please note that payment is contingent on meeting the project’s quality standards. Tasks that do not meet the **required quality bar** and are not suitable for client delivery will not be eligible for payment. However, reviewers may provide feedback and request revisions. As long as the requested feedback is addressed and the task ultimately meets the quality requirements, you will remain eligible for payment. Most tasks are expected to be one-shot at the desired quality standard.

> **Q: How can I become a reviewer?**

**A:** Writers that consistently perform well (e.g., submit hard tasks that do not need reviews) and are strong community contributors (e.g., respond to questions in the **#swe-tasks-general**) will be considered for reviewers.

Please do **not** reach out to Aahil, Donna, or another member of the leadership team to ask to be a reviewer. We will reach out if there is a need / believe you are a good fit.
