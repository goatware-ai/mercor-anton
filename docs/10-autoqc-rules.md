## Annotation Quality QC

v11 · live · subject: task

Hard, annotator-controlled acceptance gates for one Anton annotation. Only concrete defects the annotator can fix block Submit for Final QC; upstream task, seed, trajectory, or attachment faults are left to the non-blocking reviewer audit.

Blocking checks for the annotator's own rubric and grading work. Fail only for concrete, locatable, annotator-fixable defects. Do not fail an annotator for missing or contradictory upstream seed, task, trajectory, model, or attachment metadata.

### A. The Rubrics The Annotator Wrote

#### 8–15 Complete, Unique, Atomic Binary Rubrics With YES As Good

`aq_a1_rubric_count_and_form` · **blocking**

The authored rubric set has the valid size and structure: count agreement, contiguous populated statements, no duplicate criteria, one objectively gradable proposition per rubric, and YES is always desirable.

- **Pass:** AUTHORED_N is between 8 and 15 inclusive, and `field_rubric_count` equals AUTHORED_N. Verifier indices are contiguous for the authored set; every statement is non-empty; no two statements are duplicates after trimming whitespace, case-folding and collapsing punctuation/spacing; every statement tests one independently gradable proposition; every statement has an objective YES/NO answer; and YES is the desirable outcome.

A conjunction is not automatically compound when its clauses describe one inseparable behavior. FAIL atomicity only when independent subclaims could receive different answers.

Emit: declared count, AUTHORED_N, index sequence, duplicate pairs, and every non-atomic or negatively polarised statement with a corrected form.

- **Fail:** AUTHORED_N is below 8 or above 15; the declared count disagrees; an authored index is missing; a statement is blank or duplicated; a statement stacks independent requirements that could split YES/NO; a statement is open-ended; or YES describes the undesirable outcome. Quote each offending statement.

- **Notes:** Hard structural gate. Do not require 15 exactly: 8–15 is valid. Do not penalize legitimate repeated words or closely related but independently gradable criteria. Only flag empty rubric decision reasons if there are not enough YES/NO and complete reason pairs to make the stated rubric count. Extra YES/NO pairs with blank reasoning boxes are OK.

#### Each Rubric Maps To A Dimension And Has A Legitimate Source

`aq_a2_rubric_sourcing` · **blocking**

Rubrics are derived from the prompt, the environment, or common engineering knowledge - not invented.

- **Pass:** Every rubric names one of dimensions 2-9 as its mapped dimension, and its subject traces to exactly one of the three legitimate sources:

1. **Explicitly stated in the prompt** - a constraint, architectural instruction or non-functional requirement written into the task prompt.
2. **Implicitly inferable from the environment** - a project-specific pattern, existing utility module, logging framework or exception hierarchy present in the repository.
3. **Common engineering knowledge** - a principle a senior engineer would apply to this context. In production software it is common knowledge that a hacky, unreadable, unmaintainable change to core code used broadly is unacceptable; in an MVP or demo it is common knowledge that over-abstraction and multi-layered enterprise complexity are unacceptable.

Emit: the dimension histogram, and for each rubric a one-line source attribution naming which of the three it came from and the evidence for it.

- **Fail:** A rubric has no mapped dimension, or is invented - it asserts a requirement with no referent in the prompt, nothing in the bundle to infer it from, and no standing as general senior-engineering practice for this context. Quote it, say which of the three sources you tested it against, and what you looked for.

- **Notes:** Allocation across dimensions is the annotator's judgement; an uneven histogram is not a defect on its own.

#### Every Rubric Answered For Both Sides With A Reason

`aq_a3_rubric_answers_both_sides` · **blocking**

Each authored rubric carries a YES/NO for side A and side B, each with a reason citing what was observed.

- **Pass:** For every authored rubric, both `..._answer_a` and `..._answer_b` are set and both reasons are non-empty and specific - naming a file, symbol, command or step rather than restating the rubric.

Emit: any rubric index missing an answer or a reason on either side.

- **Fail:** An authored rubric is unanswered on either side, a reason is blank, or a reason merely repeats the rubric text or says 'yes it did'. Quote the weakest two.

- **Notes:** Answers beyond the number actually authored are optional slots and must be ignored, not flagged.

#### Gemini Rubric Pass Rate Is Strictly Below 75%

`aq_a4_gemini_pass_rate` · **blocking**

The Gemini side passes fewer than 75% of the authored rubrics, matching the client's strict acceptance criterion.

- **Pass:** Resolve the Gemini side from `field_gemini_side` and cross-check it against `field_model_a` / `field_model_b`. For that side, count YES answers over rubric indices 1..AUTHORED_N. PASS only when YES_COUNT / AUTHORED_N < 0.75. Report the fraction, percentage and indices answered YES.

- **Fail:** The recorded Gemini side is valid and complete, and YES_COUNT / AUTHORED_N is greater than or equal to 0.75. Exactly 75.0% fails. Tell the annotator to improve the task-specific, discriminative rubric set and then re-answer it from evidence; never instruct them to flip truthful answers merely to lower the rate.

- **Neutral:** Gemini side/model mapping is absent or ambiguous, or one of the Gemini-side authored rubric answers is missing. Those upstream/missing-input conditions are reported by advisory/reviewer QC rather than blamed on the annotator here.

- **Notes:** Client acceptance threshold is strict <75%. Count only authored rubrics 1..AUTHORED_N.

### B. Dimension Scores And Their Reasons

#### Dimensions 2-9 Scored On Both Sides With A Final Quality Score

`aq_b1_coverage` · **blocking**

Full coverage of the eight graded dimensions for each rollout, plus a final quality score per side.

- **Pass:** All of `field_ta_dim2..dim9_score_a` and `_b` are set, each has a non-empty reason, and `field_ta_final_quality_score_a` and `_b` are both set.

Emit: any missing score or reason by dimension and side.

- **Fail:** Any of the sixteen scores or sixteen reasons is missing, or either final quality score is unset. List them.

- **Neutral:** The annotation is entirely empty - the task has not been worked. Say so and stop.

- **Notes:** Dimension 1 is retired and Dimension 0 is seeded; neither is the annotator's to fill. Do not flag them.

#### The Score Agrees With Its Own Reason

`aq_b3_score_reason_agreement` · **blocking**

The number and the justification next to it describe the same quality of work.

- **Pass:** For every dimension and side, the reason's content is consistent with the score chosen: high scores carry reasons describing clean work, low scores carry reasons naming real defects.

Emit: every dimension where the reason and the number point in different directions, quoting both.

- **Fail:** A reason describing a substantive defect sits next to a 4 or 5, or a reason describing clean, verified work sits next to a 1 or 2, with nothing reconciling the two. Quote the pair.

- **Neutral:** The annotation is empty.

- **Notes:** This is the most common real defect. Be concrete: name the dimension, the side, the score and the phrase that contradicts it.

#### Dimension 6 Actually Audited Over-Researching And Telescoping

`aq_b5_over_research_audited` · **blocking**

The annotator engaged with wasted turns, thrashing and telescoping when scoring Planning, Research and Tool Proficiency.

- **Pass:** The dimension 6 reason on each side addresses search and planning efficiency explicitly - either naming redundant searches, repeated reads, thrashing or telescoping into an ad-hoc approach without scoping alternatives, or stating positively that exploration was proportionate to the task's difficulty.

Emit: the dimension 6 score and the clause that carries the efficiency finding, per side.

- **Fail:** A dimension 6 reason on either side is silent on research efficiency and planning - it scores the dimension without engaging with the behaviour the dimension exists to measure. Quote the reason.

- **Neutral:** Dimension 6 is unscored on that side.

- **Notes:** Judge only whether the ANNOTATOR audited it and recorded the finding. Do not form your own view of whether the agent over-researched - that is the separate task and trajectory QC, not this module.

#### Dimension 3 Actually Audited Diff Cleanliness

`aq_b6_surgical_patch_audited` · **blocking**

The annotator engaged with duplication, extraneous reformatting, unsolicited refactoring and dead code when scoring Codebase Conventions & Utility Reuse.

- **Pass:** The **Dimension 3** reason on each side addresses the shape of the change explicitly - either naming duplicated logic, gratuitous reformatting, unrequested refactoring, leftover debug statements, dead code or unused imports, or stating positively that the diff was surgical and every touched line traced to the task.

Emit: the dimension 3 score and the clause carrying the diff finding, per side.

- **Fail:** The Dimension 3 reason is silent on diff shape and utility reuse on either side - it scores conventions without engaging with duplication or diff cleanliness. Quote the reason.

- **Neutral:** Dimension 3 is unscored on that side.

- **Notes:** Diff cleanliness lives in **Dimension 3 (Codebase Conventions & Utility Reuse, Anti-Duplication)** in this world's taxonomy - its own description covers surgical diffs, dead code and unused imports. The vendor guide's auditor checklist calls this 'Dimension 4' because that checklist uses the JSON-report numbering, where dim 4 is Change Discipline. Do NOT look for this in Dimension 4 here; dim 4 is Robustness, Safety & API Stability. Judge only whether the annotator audited it, never whether their finding was right.

#### Dimension 7 Actually Audited Test Integrity

`aq_b7_test_integrity_audited` · **blocking**

The annotator engaged with test weakening when scoring Verification and Testing Discipline.

- **Pass:** The dimension 7 reason on each side addresses verification evidence and test integrity explicitly - either naming deleted, disabled, weakened or hardcoded tests, or stating positively that no test weakening was observed and what verification was actually run.

Emit: the dimension 7 score and the clause carrying the integrity finding, per side.

- **Fail:** A dimension 7 reason on either side is silent on test integrity and verification evidence. Quote it.

- **Neutral:** Dimension 7 is unscored on that side.

- **Notes:** The guide is zero-tolerance on test weakening, so silence here is a real gap in the annotation. Still, judge only the annotation - do not inspect the tests yourself.

#### The Annotation Does Not Admit Required Evidence Was Skipped

`aq_b8_required_evidence_reviewed` · **blocking**

The annotator actually reviewed the evidence needed for the scores and claims they recorded.

- **Pass:** No rubric reason, dimension reason or comparative rationale says that a required trajectory, patch/diff, test result, command output or final message was not reviewed, unavailable, or merely assumed while still assigning a substantive score or claim that depends on it.

- **Fail:** The annotator explicitly says required evidence was not examined, could not be accessed, was unavailable, or was assumed, yet still records a score, YES/NO answer or comparative claim that depends on that evidence. Quote the admission and identify the dependent fields that must be revisited.

- **Neutral:** The text does not expressly establish whether evidence was reviewed. Do not infer non-review from silence; other grounding checks handle unsupported claims.

- **Notes:** Fail only on an explicit, concrete admission or contradiction. This is not a prose-style test.

### C. Pairwise Verdict Consistency

#### Preference Label Exactly Matches Final-Score Direction And Magnitude

`aq_c1_preference_matches_scores` · **blocking**

The pairwise label is the deterministic label derived from the final quality score gap.

- **Pass:** Let GAP = final A minus final B. The exact mapping is: GAP=0 -> TIE; GAP=1 -> A_BETTER; GAP>=2 -> A_MUCH_BETTER; GAP=-1 -> B_BETTER; GAP<=-2 -> B_MUCH_BETTER.

Emit both final scores, GAP, expected label and recorded label.

- **Fail:** The recorded label differs from the exact mapping above, including a _MUCH_BETTER label for only a one-point gap or a plain _BETTER label for a gap of two or more.

- **Neutral:** Either final quality score or the preference is unset. The separate completeness gate handles the missing field.

- **Notes:** Hard arithmetic check; do not substitute qualitative judgment for the score-gap mapping.

#### The Comparative Rationale Reflects The Findings Actually Recorded

`aq_c2_rationale_grounded` · **blocking**

The rationale argues from the per-dimension reasons in this annotation, not from generic preference language.

- **Pass:** The comparative rationale names concrete differences that appear in the per-dimension reasons or the rubric answers, and points the same way as the preference label.

Emit: the dimensions the rationale leans on, and whether each is supported by the reason recorded for it.

- **Fail:** The rationale is generic enough to fit any pair, cites a difference that no dimension reason or rubric answer supports, or argues for the side the label did not pick. Quote it.

- **Neutral:** The rationale is unset.

- **Notes:** The rationale does not have to mention every dimension - only that what it does claim is supported.

### D. Grounding And Integrity

#### Claims Refer To Things The Package Actually Contains

`aq_d1_claims_anchored` · **blocking**

Files, symbols, tests and commands cited by the annotator exist in this task.

- **Pass:** Every specific referent named in the dimension reasons, rubric reasons and rationale can be found in the task bundle or is plainly part of the recorded work.

Emit: any cited path or symbol you could not locate.

- **Fail:** A reason cites a file, function or test that does not exist in this task - a hallucinated referent. Quote the claim and say what you searched.

- **Neutral:** The annotation is empty, or the bundle is not available to check against. Say which.

- **Notes:** Be careful before failing this: a referent may exist under a different path in the bundle. Search before you call it absent, and say where you looked.

#### Required Annotation Fields Have The Delivery Schema And Allowed Values

`aq_d4_report_conformance` · **blocking**

All required authored rubric answers, dimension scores/reasons, final scores, preference and comparative rationale are present and correctly typed.

- **Pass:** For rubric indices 1..AUTHORED_N, both answers are exactly YES or NO and both reasons are non-empty. Dimensions 2–9 on both sides have a score in the allowed 1–5 scale and a non-empty reason. Both final quality scores are in the allowed 1–5 scale. Preference is exactly one of A_MUCH_BETTER, A_BETTER, TIE, B_BETTER, B_MUCH_BETTER. Comparative rationale is non-empty.

Emit every missing or invalid field by exact path.

- **Fail:** Any required field above is blank, has the wrong type, or contains a value outside its allowed set. Do not fail optional rubric slots beyond AUTHORED_N.

- **Neutral:** The annotation is entirely empty; report that once.

- **Notes:** Shape and allowed values only. Do not double-report semantic weakness here.

## Annotation Quality QC (Reviewers)

v8 · live · subject: task

Non-blocking post-approval Anton audit. Rechecks the annotation, both sides and available task/trajectory/patch evidence from the client's perspective, and flags anything that could merit a vendor send-back. It never gates or reverses reviewer approval.

Non-blocking post-approval Anton audit. Rechecks the annotation, both sides and available task/trajectory/patch evidence from the client's perspective, and flags anything that could merit a vendor send-back. It never gates or reverses reviewer approval.

### A. Field Completeness

#### A1 - Every Authored Rubric Has A Statement

`rq_a1_rubric_slots_complete` · **non_blocking**

Each prompt-specific rubric verifier carries a usable criterion.

- **Pass:** For every entry in `task_verifiers` with config_id `evalcfg_prompt_specific_rubric`, `config_input.criteria` is non-empty after stripping and longer than 15 characters.

Emit a table: verifier_index | verifier_id | criteria present | first 80 chars.

- **Fail:** Any rubric verifier has an absent, empty or whitespace-only `config_input.criteria`, or a criteria string under 15 characters. List the verifier_index and verifier_id.

- **Neutral:** `task_verifiers` is absent or empty in the resolved subject. Say so once and mark sections A-C neutral rather than failing every rubric dimension.

- **Notes:** AUTHORED_N comes from this list, not from a guess. If `field_rubric_count` disagrees with the count here, report both in A2 — do not silently prefer one.

#### A2 - field_rubric_count Matches The Authored Verifiers

`rq_a2_rubric_count_agrees` · **non_blocking**

The declared rubric count and the actual number of rubric verifiers agree.

- **Pass:** `custom_fields.field_rubric_count`, read as an integer, equals AUTHORED_N (the number of `evalcfg_prompt_specific_rubric` entries in `task_verifiers`), and AUTHORED_N is at least 8.

Emit: the declared count, AUTHORED_N, and whether they agree.

- **Fail:** The declared count differs from AUTHORED_N, or AUTHORED_N is below 8. Give both numbers.

- **Neutral:** Either `field_rubric_count` or `task_verifiers` is absent.

- **Notes:** The floor is 8 and the ceiling is 15. Do not penalise a set of 15.

#### A3 - Every Authored Rubric Answered On Both Sides

`rq_a3_rubric_answers_both_sides` · **non_blocking**

Slots 1..AUTHORED_N each carry a YES/NO and a reason for A and for B.

- **Pass:** For every NN in 01..AUTHORED_N, all four of `field_ta_rubric_NN_answer_a`, `_answer_b`, `_reason_a`, `_reason_b` are present and non-empty, and each answer is exactly YES or NO.

Emit: any slot missing an answer or a reason, by side.

- **Fail:** A slot within 1..AUTHORED_N is unanswered on either side, a reason is blank, or an answer is a value other than YES or NO. List each.

- **Neutral:** `task_verifiers` is absent so AUTHORED_N cannot be established.

- **Notes:** Answer fields BEYOND AUTHORED_N are unused slots in a fixed 25-slot schema. Their absence is correct and must be ignored, never flagged.

#### A4 - Dimensions 2-9 Scored And Reasoned On Both Sides

`rq_a4_dimension_coverage` · **non_blocking**

Full coverage of the eight graded dimensions per rollout, plus a final quality score.

- **Pass:** For D in 2..9, `field_ta_dimD_score_a`, `_score_b`, `_reason_a` and `_reason_b` are all present and non-empty, and `field_ta_final_quality_score_a` and `_b` are both set.

Emit: any missing score or reason, by dimension and side.

- **Fail:** Any of the sixteen scores or sixteen reasons is missing, or either final quality score is unset. List them.

- **Neutral:** The annotation is entirely empty — the task has not been worked. Say so and stop.

- **Notes:** Dimension 1 is RETIRED and Dimension 0 is SEEDED. `field_ta_dim1_*` and `field_part1_reason` are not the annotator's to fill — never flag their absence.

#### A5 - Preference Label And Comparative Rationale Present

`rq_a5_verdict_fields_present` · **non_blocking**

The pairwise verdict cells hold values of the right shape.

- **Pass:** `field_e511999a150a45c2b7145f808103d78c` is exactly one of A_MUCH_BETTER, A_BETTER, TIE, B_BETTER, B_MUCH_BETTER; `field_1e32bd048d564685ba8ec51f9af11c70` is non-empty and at least 200 characters after stripping; `field_part1_a` and `field_part1_b` are each PASS, PARTIAL or FAIL.

Emit each value.

- **Fail:** The label is outside the five permitted strings, the rationale is blank or under 200 characters, or a Dimension 0 cell is outside {PASS, PARTIAL, FAIL}. Name each.

- **Neutral:** The annotation is empty.

- **Notes:** `field_part1_reason` is NOT part of this check. It is absent on every task in this world and is not captured by the export — failing on it produced a MAJOR error on every task in a previous batch.

### B. Rubric Construction

#### B1 - Each Rubric Is Binary With YES As The Good Answer

`rq_b1_binary_and_polarised` · **non_blocking**

Every criterion is objectively answerable YES/NO, and YES is always the desirable outcome.

- **Pass:** Each `config_input.criteria` is a closed question answerable YES or NO from the recorded evidence, and its polarity is right: YES marks desirable behaviour, NO marks the defect.

Correct: 'Did the agent reuse RetryPolicy from src/common/retries.py?' — YES is good.
Incorrect: 'Did the agent write a duplicate retry loop?' — YES marks a defect; it must be reframed.

Emit: any criterion where YES would be the bad outcome, quoted, with the inverted phrasing.

- **Fail:** A criterion is open-ended, not objectively answerable, or negatively framed so that YES marks a defect. Quote every offending statement with the correction.

- **Neutral:** `task_verifiers` is absent.

#### B2 - Rubrics Test The Requirement, Not One Implementation's Incidental Choices

`rq_b2_not_implementation_locked` · **non_blocking**

A rubric must not encode a detail the prompt left free, such that a compliant alternative scores NO.

- **Pass:** Each criterion tests something the prompt actually requires, or a real engineering principle — not an arbitrary choice one rollout happened to make.

The test: if a competent solution satisfying the prompt would score NO on this rubric, the rubric is implementation-locked.

Emit: any criterion that pins a detail the prompt left open, with the prompt language that does or does not require it.

- **Fail:** A rubric encodes parameter ORDER, a specific identifier name, or another free choice that the prompt does not mandate, and the rubric is answered NO for a rollout that met the prompt's actual requirement. Quote the criterion, quote the prompt, and name the rollout penalised.

Report as a warning for a human — this is a rubric-authoring defect, not a scoring error.

- **Neutral:** `task_verifiers` or `field_task_prompt` is absent.

- **Notes:** Worked example from this world: the prompt says RotationTime.__init__ should 'accept the target weekday alongside the target time of day' — it does NOT mandate which comes first. A rubric phrased 'accept target_day BEFORE target_time' therefore marks a prompt-compliant rollout NO purely for ordering. Non-blocking: flag it, do not gate.

#### B3 - Declared Source And Mapped Dimension

`rq_b3_rubric_source_declared` · **non_blocking**

Each rubric declares which dimension it maps to and where the requirement came from.

- **Pass:** For each rubric verifier, `verifier_custom_field_values.mapped_dimension` names one of dimensions 2-9, and `verifier_custom_field_values.rubric_source` is one of EXPLICIT_PROMPT, INFERRABLE_FROM_ENVIRONMENT, COMMON_ENGINEERING_KNOWLEDGE.

Emit: per verifier_index, the mapped dimension and declared source.

- **Fail:** A rubric verifier carries a `verifier_custom_field_values` dict that HAS these keys but with an empty or out-of-enum value. Name the verifier_index and the bad value.

- **Neutral:** `verifier_custom_field_values` is an empty dict `{}` on the rubric verifiers — which is its state on every verifier in this world. The fields are declared in the world's verifier schema but are not populated in the export, so neither the mapping nor the source can be read. Go NEUTRAL for all slots, say it once, and record it under Z1.

- **Notes:** DO NOT look for `field_ta_rubric_01_source` … `_15_source` in task custom_fields. Those ids do not exist in the schema and never have. Reporting them as 'absent from the resolved task custom fields' is a false positive against the annotator, who recorded the source in Studio on the verifier. This exact error produced a MAJOR failure on a whole batch.

#### B4 - Rubric Dependencies Point At Real Prerequisites

`rq_b4_dependencies_coherent` · **non_blocking**

Where a rubric declares verifier_dependencies, the referenced rubric exists and is a genuine prerequisite.

- **Pass:** Every id in `verifier_dependencies` resolves to another entry's `verifier_id` on this task, and the dependency reads as a real precondition — a rubric about a call site depending on the rubric that defines the signature it calls.

Emit: each dependency edge as index -> index, with a one-line judgement.

- **Fail:** A dependency names a verifier_id absent from this task, or a rubric depends on itself. Name the edge.

- **Neutral:** No rubric declares dependencies — that is normal and not a defect.

- **Notes:** An empty dependency list is the common, correct case. Never require dependencies.

### C. Reason Substance And Score Agreement

#### C1 - Each Reason Names Something Particular

`rq_c1_reason_substance` · **non_blocking**

Reasons cite concrete evidence rather than paraphrasing the scoring band.

- **Pass:** Each dimension reason names a file, function, symbol, command, diff hunk or step, and says what about it drove the score. A reader who disagreed would know where to look.

Emit: the two weakest reasons, quoted, with what is missing from each.

- **Fail:** A reason restates band language ('minor issues', 'solid approach'), is a single generic clause, or would read identically against a different task. Quote every such reason.

### E. Grounding And Fabrication

#### E1 - Every Claim About A Rollout Matches That Rollout's Recorded Diff

`rq_e1_claims_match_the_diff` · **non_blocking**

Assertions about what an agent did or did not change are checked against field_patch_a and field_patch_b.

- **Pass:** For every factual claim a reason or rubric answer makes about a rollout's code changes, the corresponding diff supports it.

READ THE DIFF PROPERLY. In a unified diff, a line beginning `-` was REMOVED by that patch and a line beginning `+` was ADDED. A hunk header `@@ -old,N +new,M @@` states how many lines the region held before and after. A claim that code was 'retained' or 'left in place' is FALSE if the diff shows those lines with a `-` prefix.

Emit: for each side, every claim you checked, the hunk you checked it against, and whether it held.

- **Fail:** A reason or rubric answer asserts something the diff contradicts. Quote the annotator's sentence, quote the contradicting hunk, and state what the diff actually does.

Treat this as a serious finding: a fabricated defect drives the dimension score, the rubric answer AND the pairwise preference, so one invented claim can corrupt the whole annotation. Enumerate every cell that inherits it.

- **Neutral:** `field_patch_a` and `field_patch_b` are both absent or empty, so no claim can be checked. Say so and go neutral for this dimension only.

- **Notes:** WORKED EXAMPLE from this world, which this dimension exists to catch. The loop was DELETED. The claimed defect did not exist, and it propagated into dim2/3/4/5/9 reason_a, rubric_01_answer_a, rubric_02_reason_a and the comparative rationale — scoring one rollout 2 and the other 5. Always open the diff.

## Task Auditor 7.1 Checklist QC

First distinguish required behavior from an actual departure using the supplied task context.

#### VE07 - Pass/fail-only comparison

`awqc_ve07` · **non_blocking**

A preference explanation interprets behavioral differences rather than only comparing scores or test counts.

- **Pass:** PASS when the COMPLETE preference explanation, including its clearly linked supporting reasons, identifies a relevant behavioral or engineering difference and connects it to the preference. Scores, test counts, a tally or a short result sentence may coexist with that analysis. A standalone result table is not itself a preference explanation. This criterion tests whether the justification is ONLY scores/counts/status, not whether any such numbers appear.
Before deciding, locate the preference conclusion and read all of its supporting text. In a PASS explanation, quote a concise substantive supporting passage and identify its connection to the preference. One genuinely relevant reason defeats a score-only allegation for that explanation, even if another sentence is weak or a separate inference needs a different rule. Continue checking any distinct preference explanations actually present.

- **Fail:** FAIL when a complete preference explanation is justified ONLY by scores, test counts or pass/fail status, with no relevant behavioral interpretation in its supplied or explicitly linked support. Quote the complete comparison or identify its complete field and provide faithful excerpts that do not hide resolving context. State what interpretation is missing.
FAIL control: "A wins because it passed 9 tests and B passed 6." Changing this to "A is closer" does not supply behavioral interpretation.
PASS control: "A passed 9 tests and B passed 6. A validates the key before mutation; B changes the row before checking it, leaving partial state on rejection. I prefer A because it avoids that partial state." The first sentence is numerical, but the full preference is not score-only.
A comparison discussing helper reuse, error-handling behavior, verification practice or a supported maintainability/communication trade-off must be evaluated in full. Do not select an opening score summary, ignore the subsequent explanation and label the whole field test-only. If the complete or linked support is unavailable, report the specific REVIEW_CANDIDATE or REVIEW_LIMITATION, not a confirmed absence of reasoning. Do not require a new test or independently regrade the agents.

### Context-Dependent Flags - Unnecessary wording

#### UN01 - Removable filler

`awqc_un01` · **non_blocking**

The annotation contains no filler whose removal would improve directness without losing useful meaning.

- **Pass:** The suspected wording contributes useful meaning, qualification, structure or a relevant connection between evidence and judgment, or deletion would not substantively improve the annotation. The existence of a separate YES/NO or score field does not by itself make a corresponding phrase in a rationale filler. A phrase is not filler merely because it can be deleted grammatically or because a shorter version exists. Establish a substantive gain in directness while preserving the explanation's relevant function.

- **Fail:** Flag a specific span that adds no useful information and whose deletion improves directness without changing meaning, qualification or evidential strength. Quote the span and show the sentence after deletion, or explain exactly what improves. "It is worth noting that A ran test.sh twice" can become "A ran test.sh twice".

YES/NO prefixes and concluding score statements are not automatically violations. They can be redundant, but can also orient a standalone reason or connect an explained limitation to a score. Establish actual redundant framing; use [REVIEW_CANDIDATE] when that function is genuinely uncertain. A substantive limitation followed by "so the score remains 4" is not a confirmed filler defect merely because another field also contains 4.

Do not classify an entire substantive observation as filler just because its significance needs more explanation. Missing support and redundant wording are different issues. Preserve meaningful qualifications such as "may", "only" and "in the supplied record". Assess only this dimension; do not invent text or other graders' decisions.
For every proposed filler finding, apply a deletion test in the native explanation: identify the exact span, show the revised sentence or passage, state what function is lost, and explain why that function adds no useful meaning here. A factual limitation followed by "so the score remains 4" can explicitly connect evidence to evaluation. A YES prefix can orient a standalone rationale. Similar endings across different score fields do not establish copied filler when the evidence is independently specific.
Do not use a P01 punctuation violation to prove the word YES is filler, or classify an entire meaningful observation as filler because its implication needs explanation. When redundancy is concretely plausible but the orienting/evaluative function remains unresolved, use REVIEW_CANDIDATE with the precise uncertainty. Mere personal preference for brevity is not enough.

### Context-Dependent Flags - Illogical wording

#### IL01 - Contradictory statements

`awqc_il01` · **non_blocking**

Claims about the same side, condition and version are mutually consistent.

- **Pass:** The annotation’s claims are consistent after accounting for side, condition, time and version. Review within each field AND across related annotations from this task. A file-by-file pass that never compares linked claims is not enough for an unqualified consistency PASS.

- **Fail:** Flag incompatible claims about the same event or condition without a stated scope distinction. Quote both claims, or the complete self-conflicting sentence, and check side/time/version before deciding. If meaning or scope is unresolved, report [REVIEW_CANDIDATE]. “Fails gracefully with no errors.” is an unresolved failure-behavior concern, not proof that graceful failure requires an unhandled error. Do not verify the underlying execution history from excluded files.
Make explicit comparisons of relevant claims about (a) reading/editing/execution chronology, (b) the same failed check and final outcome, (c) features described as named versus unmentioned, (d) an assertion change described as spec-driven versus output-fitting, and (e) quantitative metrics. Only report a pattern when actual supplied quotes support it.
CONFIRMED example: "Steps 2 through 12 are all reading, before any code is written" versus "Step 10 writes the implementation," for the same run and numbering system. The interval includes the claimed edit. Flag the inconsistent wording; do not guess which step is correct.
Metric example: a file reported as +240/-80 elsewhere is said to "grow by 240 lines." Those stated counts imply net growth of 160; distinguish additions from growth without independently recounting the patch. Preserve any uncertainty in the source numbers.
Qualification examples: "No step records a pass" versus "the last message says tests passed" may distinguish recorded output from a summary assertion. "The summary lists the feature" versus "it does not disclose it as a departure" can be consistent. Conversely, "the feature goes unmentioned" may leave that distinction unclear. Report REVIEW_CANDIDATE when a supported scope or attribution interpretation might resolve the conflict. Do not equate unrelated step and message indices, distinct test attempts, versions or sides. A spec-driven assertion correction is not automatically test weakening; check the linked criterion and stated reason.

#### IL02 - Conclusion not supported by its premise

`awqc_il02` · **non_blocking**

A conclusion follows from the premise offered to support it.

- **Pass:** The stated premise supports the conclusion in the way the annotation claims.

- **Fail:** Flag a conclusion presented as following from a fact that does not establish it. Quote premise and conclusion and identify the missing link. “The hidden tests pass on both sides, so B’s patch is incomplete” does not establish incompleteness. “Neither side passes, so A wins on correctness” does not establish a winner. Check supplied additional explanation, but do not invent it. This evaluates the inference, not the factual truth of the premise.
For each relevant conclusion, identify its stated premise and the exact missing connection. Check the whole rationale, its mapped criterion, related annotations and the supplied prompt when the claim depends on task scope. The prompt is context even though it is not an authored-writing target.
Scope calibration: a prompt explicitly incorporates a complete protocol and names recovery/replay. "The issue title mentions two symptoms, so any recovery work is unrequested" does not establish a scope violation. Likewise, an implementation identifier not literally named in the prompt can implement a required behavior. Ask what behavior exceeds the full incorporated contract; do not decide that every added detail was necessary, harmless or correct, and do not change scores.
Other possible support mismatches need context, not automatic failure: a recorded gate PASS versus unrestricted harmlessness; executing checks in logs versus naming them in the final summary; or describing an alleged equality/hash inconsistency as solely a performance issue. A clear supplied definition or explicit supporting link can resolve these. Use REVIEW_CANDIDATE when the intended bound, artifact link or consequence remains genuinely uncertain. Evaluate the writing's inference rather than independently proving code semantics.
A scope or evidence claim repeated across dimensions, rubric reasons and the comparison must be reviewed at every actual location. Group the recurring inference when useful but preserve affected fields. Do not relabel meaningful substantive observations as UN01 filler or turn a comparison with other behavioral support into a VE07 score-only failure.

### Review coverage

#### Z01 - Input coverage and unresolved attribution

`awqc_z01` · **non_blocking**

The supplied annotation view is readable and attributable; visible input limitations are surfaced.
